Decision
Battery operation + ONE complete arm prioritized. Kinect v2 confirmed. Six X-marked entries are retained as already owned. No purchases made.
Budget status

Meaning of columns
Exact original 11 headers retained. Qty describes the quantity on each row, including later items; Phase 1 2027 is NEW money allocated now, not automatic subtotal. X rows cost zero new money. Subtotal is row quantity × unit value; deferred rows do not enter phase-1 total. Owned values come from your screenshot. Blank price means no defensible estimate. N/S = maximum not specified/verified.
Ratings
Unless stated otherwise ratings are per unit. PSU/converter output capacity is NOT a measured load maximum. Do not sum regulator capacity, battery power and load power as if separate loads. Sensor typical current was not substituted for an unknown maximum.
Battery correction
P42A nominal voltage is 3.6 V and typical capacity 4.2 Ah. 6S3P is therefore 21.6 V nominal, 25.2 V fully charged, 12.6 Ah typical; the original 22.2 V/12 Ah/266 Wh describes a different nominal-cell assumption. Supplier must label the finished pack and usable energy. No six-hour guarantee: 28 W from the old component mix is not an established measurement for this robot.
What phase 1 delivers
Target: existing Pi/SSD/colour camera plus one complete arm, powered from a new protected 6S pack; T12 retains its own battery/dock. Simple reused structure. Battery/stop integration and base-control qualification remain engineering work. This is not yet a finished autonomous mobile manipulator.
What is deferred
Second arm, Kinect v2 and adapter, both IMUs, extra ToF, Hailo, microphone, active audio and camera pan/tilt, telemetry, solar and custom PCB fabrication. Kinect v2 stays the intended depth camera; it has not been silently replaced by the owned colour camera.
Base integration
T12 is not a Roomba. It has its own battery and OEM motor controller. Preserve them initially. Its LiDAR/control interface, added payload, tipping stability and independent hardware stop are unqualified. Do not connect 6S to its battery terminals or 20 V dock. If OEM controls cannot be accessed, replacement electronics need additional budget.
Power structure
6S protected pack → main fuse/disconnect → separate fused Pi 5 V converter and actuator-stop-controlled 12 V arm converter. T12 battery → OEM T12 electronics, with separately engineered drive stop. Common signal references only as required by final interfaces; do not parallel battery positives. Pi USB/Wi-Fi → arm controller. Pi CSI → existing camera. Future Kinect USB3 + dedicated 12 V branch. No new servo PWM board required for RoArm.
Pi supply decision
The lower-cost proposed Pololu route uses a rated, fused GPIO 5 V/GND harness, not a USB-C PD adapter. Requires polarity/voltage-drop/thermal qualification, no simultaneous USB-C power, and correct firmware current configuration after validating supply capacity. This is a schematic design hold, not beginner wiring instructions. A packaged Pi-compatible PD supply can replace it when budget permits.
Arm supply and mounting
RoArm-M2-S is preassembled and accepts 7–12.6 V. Use 12 V; never 6S directly. The DDR-60G-12 proposal matches the 5 A/60 W supplied adapter capacity; test moving/stall transients and temperature before release. Arm reset can cause motion. Stop design must consider falling loads. Payload/space must be checked on the actual base.
Quote sensitivity
Example only: battery quote €200 instead of €150, protection €65 instead of €40, and €20 shipping yields €674.75 including those costs (no extra reserve). That is €74.75 over budget before any deferred sensors. The spreadsheet allocation must not conceal this risk.
Buying order
First confirm T12 condition/control access and obtain the pack+charger+protection quotes. Then lock delivered costs and arm mounting. Purchase ONE completed arm if the essential total fits. Qualify converters and wiring before battery operation. No supplier contact or order has been made.
